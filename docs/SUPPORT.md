# Train Tracker Support

Train Tracker is an independent, open-source app. It is not affiliated with VIA Rail Canada and cannot help with tickets, refunds or reservations; for those, contact the railway.

## Get help or report a problem

Use the contact form: <https://junxuanb.com/#contact>

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the app version (Settings tab, bottom of the page), and what you expected to happen. Do not post personal information.

## Common questions

**The times look wrong or out of date.**
Times come from the railway's public status data and can lag behind. The trip screen shows when the data was last received. Pull down to refresh. Always confirm with station announcements.

**My train is not in the list.**
Trains appear once the railway publishes them, usually on the day. For a later date the app offers the same train with reference times borrowed from a recent run, marked "Reference timetable". Late at night the list is shorter because few trains are running.

**I saved a trip for next week and there is no countdown.**
That is intended. The countdown and the Live Activity start five hours before your planned time to leave home.

**The Live Activity disappeared.**
iOS ends a Live Activity after eight hours. The app starts a new one automatically when it can; otherwise open the trip and tap "Start Live Activity". Check Settings → Train Tracker → Live Activities if it never appears.

**I do not get notifications.**
Open the Settings tab in the app → "Notifications". Notifications are optional; the app works without them.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete the trip in the app ("…" → "Delete trip"), or delete the app. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Is there an English interface?**
Yes. English and Simplified Chinese follow the system app-language setting. Change it in iOS Settings → Apps → Train Tracker → Language. Station names stay as published by the railway.
